# Technical white paper — draft v0.1

**As of 2026-11-03:** A full draft Word document has been prepared for review separately. This repository note records the research scope, not peer-reviewed or published findings.

## Working title
PREDICT-SHIELD: An AI-Enabled Framework for Predictive Cyber Threat Intelligence, Automated Cybersecurity Risk Assessment, and Policy-Governed Adaptive Cyber Defense.

## Research contribution to test
Platform-independent evidence reconciliation; conservative four-state assurance with explicit uncertainty; risk and disruption aware, policy-authorized defensive action; independent post-action verification of security and service health.

## Experiments
- **A:** Partial vulnerability remediation, stale deployments, missing and contradictory evidence.
- **B:** Inconsistent authorization enforcement following delayed or lost permission propagation.

## Controls
Compare matched scanner/rules/OPA/fixed-remediation baselines; include ablations, dated EPSS/KEV snapshots, ground-truth labels, retained audit traces, and time/coverage/accuracy/safety/service-disruption metrics. No results are claimed yet.

## Editorial gates
1. Technical review of architecture and exact source citations.
2. Runnable integrated prototype and prespecified experiment cases.
3. Benchmark execution with transparent failures and uncertainty.
4. Revised results and limitations.
5. Independent external evaluation where feasible.
6. Release/publication decision following technical and IP review.

Primary source documents: PREDICT-SHIELD Research Plan v1.4.1; External Evaluation Protocol v0.2; Federal Priority Alignment v1.4.1. This document is not a technical result or a federal endorsement.
