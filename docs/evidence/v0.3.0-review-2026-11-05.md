## Design observations and blockers
- Initial deterministic asset collector reads declared service maps and CycloneDX JSON. These are not proof that packages are loaded at runtime.
- Graph-derived disruption is a heuristic weighted by dependent service criticality, not a measured service-level impact model.
- Benchmark's self-report baseline is deliberately naive; do not interpret the 8:0 count as comparative performance against realistic tools.
- Selection treats disruption >1 as exceeding error budget while its working disruption metric can exceed 1; clarify measurement versus normalized scoring in the technical methodology.
- Before external validation, preregister ground truth, baselines, disruption thresholds, independent verification oracles, and versioned input datasets.
